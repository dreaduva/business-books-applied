# Traction Worked Example

**Fictional teaching example.** All organizations, participants, numbers, and outcomes are invented for this exercise, not reported results or a case from the book.

## Goal and constraints

Fictional LessonLoop seeks paid pilot customers among independent tutors. The exploratory goal is to learn which route produces qualified walkthroughs and subsequent purchases. Constraints: 21 days, $150 cash, 12 founder hours. Attribution: first reported source, with multi-touch cases flagged rather than double-counted.

## Candidate map

Candidates: specialist newsletter, partner demonstration, search content, referrals, and paid search. Shortlist newsletter and a partner session because access is available within the window. Draft a search article but defer judgment on organic acquisition beyond this short period. Referrals lack a current customer base; paid search costs remain unknown.

## Bounded tests

Use the same pilot offer and walkthrough page. Newsletter cap: $100 and 3 hours. Partner session cap: $0 and 5 hours. Reserve 4 hours for follow-up and review. A qualified walkthrough is attended by a tutor currently managing changing lessons manually. Paid customer means an actual pilot payment, not an expression of interest. Review on day 21.

## Results and calculations

Invented outcomes: newsletter costs $100, takes 3 hours, generates 40 visits, 4 qualified walkthroughs, and 1 paying pilot. Partner session takes 5 hours, reaches 12 attendees, produces 3 qualified walkthroughs, and 1 paying pilot. Newsletter cash cost per paying pilot is $100; partner cash cost is $0, with 5 hours of work. These are exploratory observed costs, not stable CAC estimates. Follow-up uses 3 additional shared hours. Repeatability and retention remain unknown.

## Decision and next test

Do not declare the partner route a scalable winner from one purchase. Repeat a comparable partner session if another suitable partner agrees, because cash is constrained and relevance appeared promising. Proposed next cap: 5 hours; no spend authorized. Track whether the new audience is similarly qualified. If access requires extensive negotiation, reassess the time cost. Keep the newsletter result for a future offer comparison.
