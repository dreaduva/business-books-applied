# Deep Work Worked Example

**Fictional teaching example.** All organizations, participants, numbers, and outcomes are invented for this exercise, not reported results or a case from the book.

## Output and first action

Fictional Maya needs an onboarding decision memo comparing assisted setup with self-service setup. Output: one page describing the tradeoff, current evidence, and a recommendation. The task requires comparing notes and delivery constraints. First action: read the pilot-scope notes and list the two options. Inputs are prepared beforehand.

## Feasible session

Proposed Tuesday 09:00–09:45 session during a period without scheduled customer calls. Maya remains responsible for genuine account-access incidents via the established urgent channel. Routine email waits until 09:45. Forty-five minutes is a scenario-specific starting allocation, not a universal recommendation. Scope excludes redesigning the entire onboarding product.

## Environment and attention

Keep the decision document, pilot notes, and capacity estimate open. Close unrelated feeds and routine inbox tabs. Leave the urgent channel available under existing operating expectations. Capture unrelated ideas in a short scratch list, then return to the comparison. Use the internet only if a specific missing fact is essential.

## Interruptions and shutdown

A genuine customer access failure interrupts the session. Before switching when feasible, note the current comparison and next sentence to write. On return, resume from that note or schedule the remaining subtask within normal work hours. At 09:45, save the memo, record open questions, and reopen routine communications. Do not silently extend into a customer commitment.

## Review and next session

Invented outcome: 30 focused minutes, followed by a 15-minute access incident. The memo contains the comparison but lacks a decision because setup-time evidence is missing. Next action: obtain that evidence through the normal process, then use a shorter decision session. The interrupted block is not scored as a character failure, and the unfinished memo is not labeled complete.
