# $100M Offers Worked Example

**Fictional teaching example.** All organizations, participants, numbers, and outcomes are invented for this exercise, not reported results or a case from the book.

## Buyer and desired progress

Fictional LessonLoop pilot: an independent tutor wants to see unresolved lesson changes without repeatedly searching messages. Current alternative is a spreadsheet and message threads. This buyer need is a hypothesis from the teaching scenario, not established market demand.

## Value and proof

Offer a configured pending-confirmation list, demonstrated during setup. Proposed first useful result: the tutor can review their own active changes at the end of setup. Customer supplies lesson-change information and manually records replies. No claim of improved attendance or saved hours. The prototype demonstration supports only the visible tracking behavior.

## Scope and terms to review

Proposed four-week pilot at $60: one 30-minute setup call, access to the tracker, and up to 30 minutes of support. Excludes billing, automated reminders, and student records. Tutor maintains statuses. No auto-renewal proposed. Payment and cancellation wording must be settled before launch; the brief is not a live offer or legal agreement.

## Delivery feasibility

Assume $5 direct cash cost and one hour of delivery per pilot, valued internally at $25/hour. Illustrative contribution: $60 - $5 - $25 = $30 before overhead, acquisition costs, taxes, and unexpected work. Four pilots require an estimated four hours plus a two-hour shared buffer. Six available hours make four a provisional operational cap, not fabricated scarcity. Support overruns would reduce contribution.

## Offer draft and next test

Draft: “Try LessonLoop for four weeks with a setup session and a pending-confirmation view for your changing lessons. Proposed price: $60. You record replies manually; billing and reminders are not included.” Before launch, confirm functioning access and finalize terms. Proposed test: present the scoped offer to suitable prospects after authorization and record objections, actual purchases, and support time. No sales are claimed.
