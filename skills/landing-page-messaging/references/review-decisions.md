# Reviewing a messaging decision

If the visitor expects automation but the product is manual, correct the mechanism beside the headline or demonstration. A footnote may be technically accurate but insufficient at the point of misunderstanding.

If the visitor describes the product correctly and sees no need, retain the finding as relevance evidence. Do not make the promise stronger to manufacture interest. Identify the audience/offer question that needs investigation.

If the visitor clicks expecting access but receives a request form, align the CTA, form introduction and acknowledgement. Never claim an appointment is booked when only a request was received.

For each revision, return the observed interpretation, source line, changed copy, reason, and remaining uncertainty. A small reader check discovers problems; it does not prove improved conversion. Missing results should produce a proposed check rather than an invented outcome.

A full-page deliverable includes headline, supporting line, concrete problem, mechanism, evidence placement, usage steps, access steps, CTA and form/confirmation copy when relevant. Do not force every story element to become a heading or add a second CTA without a clear supporting purpose.
