# Acquisition Channel Test Plan: Method and Judgment

## Preserve the funnel

Record exposure, visits, qualified responses, activation, and purchase as distinct events where applicable. Do not quietly change the event called a conversion between channels. If a channel yields no customers, customer acquisition cost is not zero; with spend and zero acquisitions it is not a finite observed per-customer cost.

## Include the scarce resource

Keep hours alongside cash. An optional labor valuation must name its assumed hourly rate. Do not mix a cash-only figure for one channel with a fully loaded figure for another. Show shared follow-up work separately or explain its allocation.

## Interpret unequal windows

Organic search, partnerships, and paid placements can have different delays. A short test can evaluate production effort or initial relevance without establishing a channel's full acquisition potential. Name what was actually learned.

## Use small results carefully

A single buyer may be unusual. An attractive observed cost supports a follow-up question, not an extrapolated revenue forecast. Repeating a bounded test can reveal access and delivery problems that were invisible in the first attempt. Do not buy ads, contact partners, or publish outreach merely because the plan contains those actions.
