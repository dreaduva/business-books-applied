# Sources and Scope

This package is an independent practical application informed by *The Mom Test* by Rob Fitzpatrick. It supports preparation, question repair, interpretation of supplied notes, and next-test design. It is not a full-book reproduction or an official author product.

- [Author's teacher resources](https://www.momtestbook.com/teachers): question quality, biased data, learning goals, commitment, and advancement.
- [Rob Fitzpatrick interviewed on SaaS Club, episode 206](https://saasclub.io/podcast/saas-idea-validation-rob-fitzpatrick/): learning versus selling and sharing customer evidence with a team.

Sources consulted are public author material and the author interview; a full-book review and human expert review are not claimed. Questions, templates, analysis tables, and fictional examples are original project material. Installation and structural checks do not demonstrate improved business outcomes.
