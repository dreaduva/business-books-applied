# Interpreting a work habit log

Identify the original opportunity rule before calculating completion. Distinguish no opportunity, missed opportunity, delayed completion and unknown. Do not remove inconvenient days retrospectively. If the rule changes, retain the old denominator and label the new version.

Inspect output quality separately from repetition. Seven completed sentences may contain only three usable observations. Report both counts with their actual definitions; a newly introduced quality criterion must not be represented as the original rule.

Diagnose clustered misses: absent cue, conflicting obligation, missing materials, action too large or no useful purpose. Propose a specific change to the supported problem, rather than a character judgment or more reminders by default.

Recovery should resume a useful action at a realistic opportunity. It does not require doubling the next task, inventing missing source material or giving up after a lapse. Preserve the user's non-working time and obligations.

Return the calculation, example outputs, diagnosis, revised cue/minimum/fallback, review window and limitations. A short trial is not evidence of automaticity. If several design changes are necessary, explain that their effects cannot be isolated by the next aggregate rate.
