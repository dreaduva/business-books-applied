# Experiment Method and Source Scope

The skill supports experiment design, critique, result analysis, and revision. Its practical procedures are in [experiment design](experiment-design.md) and [result analysis](result-analysis.md). Use the reference relevant to the supplied task rather than loading the entire package automatically.

## What the author materials support

- [Lean Startup methodology](https://theleanstartup.com/principles): uncertainty, learning, management, and the feedback loop.
- [Eric Ries — MVP introduction](https://www.startuplessonslearned.com/2009/08/minimum-viable-product-guide.html): choosing an intervention for learning, with context-dependent judgment.
- [Eric Ries — MVPs and Excellence](https://www.startuplessonslearned.com/2015/01/mvps-and-excellence.html): quality and responsible experimentation.
- [Eric Ries — LSE lecture slides](https://www.lse.ac.uk/assets/richmedia/channels/publicLecturesAndEvents/slides/20120112_1830_theLeanStartup_sl.pdf): baseline, improvement, and strategic review.

The metric-repair procedures, event-log fields, missing-data calculations, and fictional tutoring scenario are original project applications. They are not attributed as verbatim instructions or examples from the book. Full-book expert review, independent behavioral benchmarks, and real-world outcome testing are not claimed.

## Use and limits

An experiment can resolve a bounded question without settling the whole business. Keep distinctions between demand, usability, feasibility, price acceptance, and repeated value visible. Retain uncertainty about sample selection, measurement, intervention differences, and missing observations.

The [worksheet](../assets/worksheet.md) is a reusable output format. Its fields may be condensed for a small request, but the decision-critical facts should remain visible. The [worked example](worked-example.md) demonstrates an unfavorable result and a proposed revision. It does not provide empirical evidence about tutors or any actual product.
