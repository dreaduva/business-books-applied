# Designing and Repairing a Startup Experiment

This is the project's original experiment-design procedure. It supplies practical decision criteria rather than a universal test size or success threshold.

## Identify the commitment at stake

Ask what the result will change: a build, a service offer, recruitment focus, or another bounded investment. “Learn more” is not specific enough if every possible result leads to the same action. Preserve the user's investment ceiling. If the decision is already made regardless of evidence, describe the work as implementation or monitoring rather than pretending it is a discriminating experiment.

Separate the assumption from the intervention. “Build reminders” is an action. “The responsible adult will acknowledge the changed time through this process” is an assumption that behavior can challenge. Write down which related assumptions the test will leave unanswered.

## Choose an intervention for its information

| Uncertainty | Possible intervention | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the described problem real in this situation? | Incident-focused conversation or workflow observation | Does not establish purchase behavior |
| Can a person complete this interaction? | Prototype task with observation | Does not establish production reliability |
| Can the process deliver the intended outcome? | Disclosed manual service or narrow working feature | Manual success does not establish automation feasibility |
| Will the buyer accept these terms? | A real, deliverable offer under stated conditions | Purchase is not evidence of lasting retention |
| Does useful behavior repeat? | Observe later eligible opportunities | A person without another opportunity needs separate treatment |

These are possibilities, not mandatory stages. Avoid automatically choosing a landing page because it is cheap or software because the founder is a developer. Include at least one credible alternative when the choice is material, and explain why the selected test answers the question better within the supplied constraints.

## Repair vague metrics

Turn “people like it” into an observation that bears on the assumption. Define the action and its unit. Then define eligibility: who actually had a chance to act? Give the window enough meaning that late, pending, and completed observations can be separated.

A return-use claim needs a later opportunity. An event-level process metric should not be presented as an account-level retention rate. If exposure data is missing, do not infer conversion from the number of outcomes alone.

Specify a burden measure that matters to the decision. This can be operator time, error rate, support work, or another supplied constraint. Do not attach a generic guardrail merely to fill a field. Keep preparation and exception work visible when they affect feasibility.

## Establish rules without false precision

A decision rule can come from an operating promise, cost limit, comparative objective, or risk tolerance. Ask for or expose that rationale. If none exists, label a proposed threshold as provisional or design a calibration exercise. Do not supply a familiar percentage and call it validated.

Include a result that would make the team stop, change, or investigate. A rule requiring only “some interest” may be too weak to inform a substantial investment. On the other hand, demanding statistical proof from a small exploratory workflow trial confuses the purpose of that trial.

## Critique by consequence

For each material defect, return four things: the original choice, the decision error it could cause, a concrete repair, and the uncertainty that remains. Prioritize defects that invalidate interpretation over cosmetic wording.

Example: a plan measures page views to decide whether tutors will pay. The defect is a mismatch between observation and commercial assumption. Repair by either narrowing the decision to interest in the described next step or proposing an authorized test of a deliverable paid offer. Do not invent the offer's price or accept payment while drafting the repair.

If the user explicitly asks for a light review, keep it light. Multiple modes do not require producing every asset for every request.
