# Diagnose a campaign before increasing volume

Rebuild unique counts for resource use, explicit interest, qualification, held conversation and purchase. Name every rate's denominator. Keep pending follow-up distinct from rejection and retain duplicates as touches rather than extra people.

Use evidence at its actual scope. Roles observed among eight requesters do not establish roles among one hundred downloaders. A high download count cannot establish a qualified pipeline. Missing response timestamps prevent confidently attributing low attendance to audience quality.

Separate audience mismatch, weak connection to the offer, insufficient product value, absent response ownership and broken measurement. Give a concrete replacement asset or process for the supported diagnosis. If several are plausible, name the smallest check that distinguishes them.

For revisions, return the changed message/resource, the handoff owner, follow-up expectation, unchanged qualification rule, proposed measurement and remaining uncertainty. Operational repairs may be necessary alongside a content test; disclose that simultaneous changes weaken causal attribution.

Do not send, scrape or enroll anyone as part of a planning task. Preserve opt-out and requested-interaction boundaries. A generic permission statement does not replace the user's actual authorization for external actions.
