# Lead Generation Plan: Method and Judgment

## Keep the stages distinct

A large audience can produce little qualified interest. Define qualification in terms of the buyer situation and relevant intent, not whether a person can be contacted. Retain raw counts at each stage and flag duplicates before calculating totals.

## Resource quality comes first

An entry resource should complete a small useful task. Do not deliberately make it unusable to force a sales conversation. State limitations and make the next commercial step optional and clear.

## Match access to permission

Existing relationships do not automatically authorize bulk messages. A community may prohibit promotion; a requested resource does not automatically authorize ongoing marketing. The plan should identify the relevant constraints without pretending to provide a universal legal ruling. Do not scrape or send as part of drafting.

## Volume is constrained by fulfillment

Count available response slots and who owns them. If a campaign creates more requests than the team can serve, propose a transparent waitlist or a smaller test, not a promise of immediate availability. A tiny observed conversion rate is descriptive; it does not justify a confident scaled forecast.
