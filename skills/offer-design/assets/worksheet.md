## Promise review

For each promise, name the evidence, who controls the outcome, how completion is recognized and what happens if prerequisites are missing. Record actual capacity before using scarcity language. For unresolved terms, list the decision needed; do not present a draft as ready for checkout.

Save one rejected offer, the specific reason it fails, and the revised buyer-facing copy. Separate the next demand test from the delivery-feasibility check.
