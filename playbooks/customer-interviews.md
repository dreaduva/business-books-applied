# Plan Your First Customer Interviews

[Home](../README.md) · [All playbooks](README.md) · [The Mom Test guide](../books/the-mom-test/README.md)

**Use this when:** you have a customer segment and an uncertain problem, and need to prepare useful conversations.

**Finish with:** a learning objective, participant criteria, a conversation guide, and a way to separate evidence from assumptions.

## 1. Choose a decision you need to make

Write the decision in ordinary language: “Should we investigate schedule changes for independent tutors before building a calendar?” Then list what you do not yet know. Keep the decision small enough that a conversation can inform it.

## 2. Find people with relevant recent experience

Describe the situation they must have encountered. For this example, that means tutors who recently handled a lesson change, not everyone interested in education technology. Note how you will recruit and which perspectives your sample may miss.

## 3. Prepare prompts about what happened

Use the [question examples](../books/the-mom-test/README.md#customer-interview-questions-move-from-opinions-to-incidents). Begin with a specific event. Explore its sequence, existing workaround, consequences, and alternatives. Save your product explanation until it no longer interferes with learning about the existing situation.

## 4. Capture what you heard without turning it into a feature request

Copy the [interview plan and evidence log](../templates/customer-interview-plan.md). Use separate fields for observed or reported behavior, your interpretation, and unanswered questions. Do not manufacture a quote to make your notes look complete.

## 5. Choose the next investigation

After each conversation, ask what changed in your understanding. A repeated problem may justify testing an offer. An unclear story may justify a follow-up. Evidence that a current tool works well may justify changing your initial focus.

Decide what would change your mind before collecting more evidence. Avoid calling an idea validated simply because you finished the planned interviews.
