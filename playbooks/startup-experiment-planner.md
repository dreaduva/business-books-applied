# Startup Experiment Plan Playbook

[Home](../README.md) · [Book guide](../books/the-lean-startup/README.md) · [Worksheet](../books/the-lean-startup/worksheet.md)

## 1. Name the decision

Write one choice that will be affected by evidence. List the assumptions behind it, then pick the one whose failure would most change the plan.

## 2. Specify the smallest honest test

Fill the intervention and participant fields. Describe the delivered experience exactly, including any manual work. Confirm the proposed test can actually expose the risky assumption.

## 3. Define the review before launch

Write the metric unit, denominator, observation window, guardrail, and decision rules. Name the person who will record the observations. Keep proposed thresholds separate from observed results.

## 4. Capture exceptions

Log excluded, missing, and failed events. Preserve operational effort and unexpected effects alongside the headline metric.

## 5. Make the next decision

Compare results with the original rule. State whether the intervention failed, execution was unclear, or the hypothesis needs revision. End with a specific next test or a reason to stop.
