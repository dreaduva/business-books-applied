# Lead Generation Plan Playbook

[Home](../README.md) · [Book guide](../books/100m-leads/README.md) · [Worksheet](../books/100m-leads/worksheet.md)

## 1. Define the useful response

Describe the buyer situation and the next action that signals relevant interest. Keep contact counts separate from qualification.

## 2. Choose one feasible route

Use current access, budget, and time to select a route. Record permissions that must be resolved before distribution.

## 3. Build the first useful interaction

Draft the resource and introduction. Make the resource helpful without a purchase and explain the optional next step.

## 4. Prepare the handoff

Assign a reply owner, capacity, and follow-up boundary. Make opt-outs and requests visible. Do not enroll people in ongoing messages by default.

## 5. Review the full funnel

After authorized distribution, record visits or exposures, requests, qualification, attendance, and purchases. Calculate rates with explicit denominators. Change audience fit or the handoff before increasing volume when those are the actual bottlenecks.
