# Offer Design Brief Playbook

[Home](../README.md) · [Book guide](../books/100m-offers/README.md) · [Worksheet](../books/100m-offers/worksheet.md)

## 1. Describe the buyer's desired progress

Use a specific situation and current alternative. If the need is uncertain, keep the offer provisional and identify the missing evidence.

## 2. Reduce friction honestly

Review credibility, timing, and customer work. Add assistance only where it helps the outcome and the business can supply it.

## 3. Write the full scope

Fill the worksheet with deliverables, limits, exclusions, responsibilities, proposed price, and unresolved terms. Make the next step understandable without a sales explanation.

## 4. Check fulfillment

Estimate direct costs and hours. Compare the proposed customer count with available capacity and inspect an overrun scenario. Keep contribution separate from profit.

## 5. Review the offer in use

After authorized testing, record purchases and objections separately from compliments. Track delivery effort alongside demand. Revise the scope or price assumptions when the actual work differs from the plan.
